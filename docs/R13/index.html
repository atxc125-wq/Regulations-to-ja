Uniform provisions concerning the approval of vehicles of categories M, N and O with regard to braking

1 Scope 未翻訳
（要約未生成）Scope

English (Original)
This Regulation applies to vehicles of categories M2, M3, N and O with regard to braking.

2.1 Braking system 未翻訳
（要約未生成）Braking system
▼
English (Original)
"Braking system" means the combination of parts whose function is to progressively reduce the speed of a moving vehicle, bring it to a halt, or keep it stationary if it is already halted; these functions are specified in paragraph 5.1. The system comprises the control, the transmission and the brake(s) proper.
日本語訳
翻訳中...
2.2 Service braking system 未翻訳
（要約未生成）Service braking system
▼
English (Original)
"Service braking system" means the braking system which allows the driver to control the movement of the vehicle and bring it to a halt safely, quickly and effectively, whatever its conditions of loading, and whatever the upward or downward gradient on which it is standing.

2.3 Secondary braking system 未翻訳
（要約未生成）Secondary braking system
▼
English (Original)
"Secondary braking system" means the braking system which allows the driver to halt the vehicle in the event of failure of the service braking system. It does not exclude the use of a control shared with that of the service braking system.

2.4 Parking braking system 未翻訳
（要約未生成）Parking braking system
▼
English (Original)
"Parking braking system" means the braking system which allows the vehicle to be held stationary on an inclined surface even in the absence of the driver, the active parts being then held in the locked position by a purely mechanical device.

2.5 Endurance braking system 未翻訳
（要約未生成）Endurance braking system
▼
English (Original)
"Endurance braking system" means a supplementary braking system able to stabilize vehicle speed over a long downhill section, with little or no wear to the conventional friction-based braking system.

5.1 Functions of braking systems Modified 未翻訳
（要約未生成）Functions of braking systems
▼
English (Original)
The braking system of every vehicle shall fulfil the following functions: (a) service braking, enabling the movement of the vehicle to be controlled and the vehicle to be brought to a halt safely, quickly and effectively; (b) secondary braking, enabling the vehicle to be brought to a halt in the event of failure of the service braking system, though at a reduced efficiency; (c) parking braking, enabling the vehicle to be held stationary on an upward or downward gradient, in the absence of the driver, the active parts being then held in the locked position by a purely mechanical device; (d) endurance braking, where fitted, enabling the vehicle speed to be stabilized on a long downhill section.
日本語訳
翻訳中...
変更理由 (Justification): Added function (d) for endurance braking systems to align with new definition in paragraph 2.5 and updated Annex 4 requirements.
5.2 Braking surfaces Modified 未翻訳
（要約未生成）Braking surfaces
▼
English (Original)
The braking systems prescribed in paragraph 5.1 shall be so designed that they act on braking surfaces permanently connected to the wheels through components of adequate strength. Where braking effort on an axle is provided by the engine or an electric motor, a disconnect device shall be permitted provided it is driver-operable and cannot be accidentally activated.
日本語訳
翻訳中...
変更理由 (Justification): Updated to accommodate regenerative braking in electric/hybrid vehicles. Added provision for engine/motor braking disconnect.
